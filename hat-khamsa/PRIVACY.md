# Hat Khamsa (هات خمسة) — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

**In short:** Hat Khamsa has no accounts and no analytics of its own, and the game works fully offline. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure them. Buying "Pro" removes all ads.

## Data stored on your device
The app saves team names you type, your last game setup, a list of cards already played (so they don't repeat), your settings, an unfinished game and whether you bought Pro. This stays on your device, is never uploaded by us, and is deleted when you delete the app.

## Advertising (free version)
The free version shows full-screen ads between rounds or at the end of a game, and an optional ad you can watch to unlock a bonus deck for 24 hours. Ads are provided by **Google AdMob**. To serve, limit and measure ads, Google may collect:

- device and app information (such as device model, OS version and an advertising identifier),
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
- ad interactions and diagnostic data.

- **iOS tracking permission:** the app asks once, after your first finished game, whether apps may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized on that basis.
- **Consent:** where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), a Google consent form asks for your choice before personalized ads are shown.

See [how Google uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We (the developer) do not receive personal data from AdMob, only aggregated reports.

## Purchases
"Pro" is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether Pro is owned, to remove ads and unlock decks.

## Network
When online, the app downloads a small public configuration file from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) for settings and occasional announcements. This request contains no personal information. GitHub may process standard request data such as your IP address, as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children
Hat Khamsa is a party game for groups of friends and families and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
