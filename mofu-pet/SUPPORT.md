# Mofu & Friends — Help & feedback

**How do I get around the house?**
Swipe left or right, or use the arrows at the top, to visit the six rooms: living room, spa, kitchen, bedroom, playroom and garden. The shelf at the bottom shows all your friends: tap a friend who is elsewhere to bring them into the room you are in (up to four per room), or tap one who is already here to open their card.

**How do I play with my friends?**
Every friend has their own touch: tap, stroke, press and hold, or pick them up and toss them. Mofu boings and purrs, Oink loves belly rubs, Wuff learns tricks on the Play tab of its card, and Boops wobble and giggle. Shake your phone and everyone in the room reacts.

**How does the spa work?**
Bring a friend to the spa and follow the four steps: foam with the sponge, rinse with the shower, dry with the towel, then finish with the dryer. Move your finger over your friend — the faster you rub, the louder the sounds.

**Who gets fed, and why won't they eat?**
In the kitchen, drag a snack from the menu to your friend's mouth. Each friend has a secret favourite. After six snacks in an hour they are full — try again a little later.

**How do sleep sounds work?**
In the bedroom, tap "Sleep sounds" to play the mix and "Mix" to set the volume of rain, fire, ocean, purr, chimes and soft noise, plus a sleep timer. Sleep sounds keep playing with the screen off until you stop them or the timer ends.

**How do I get new friends?**
Stars come from caring and playing: the first touch of the day, finishing a spa day, favourite snacks, the two playroom games and the daily gift in the garden. When you have enough stars, the egg in the garden is ready — tap it to hatch and name your new friend. Stars are never spent, so nothing is ever lost.

**How do I unlock outfits?**
Open a friend's card and the Wardrobe tab: every locked item says how to earn it (friendship level or total stars). Everything is free.

**I can't hear any sounds.**
Check that Sounds is on in Settings and that your phone's silent switch is off. Friend sounds mix with your music and follow the silent switch; sleep sounds play like music so they also work with the screen off.
