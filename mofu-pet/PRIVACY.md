# Mofu & Friends — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

**In short: Mofu & Friends does not collect, store or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Data stored on your device
Your friends' names, coats, outfits, friendship levels, stars, journal, sleep-sound mix and your sound, haptics and motion settings are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, and they are deleted when you delete the app (or when you choose "Start over" in Settings).

## Motion sensor
The app reads the phone's motion sensor only while it is open, to notice when you shake your phone so your friends can react. The readings are used instantly on the device and never stored or sent anywhere.

## Sleep sounds
When you play sleep sounds in the bedroom, they keep playing with the screen off until you stop them or the sleep timer ends. This uses the device's audio playback only; nothing is recorded and the microphone is never used.

## Photo booth
Photos you make in the photo booth are created on your device. They are only shared if you choose to, through the standard iOS share sheet, to the app or person you pick. The app does not keep a copy and does not access your photo library.

## Network
When online, the app downloads a small public configuration file from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) to receive seasonal items, announcements and settings. This request contains no personal information. GitHub may process standard request data such as your IP address, as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
