# Oink — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

**In short: Oink does not collect, store or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking.

## Data stored on your device
Your piglet (its name, needs, level, acorns, outfits and diary counters) and your settings (sounds, vibration) are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, and they are deleted when you delete the app or choose *Start over with a new piglet* in Settings.

## Network
The pet works fully offline. When online, the app downloads a small public configuration file from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) to receive settings, seasonal items and announcements. This request contains no personal information. GitHub may process standard request data such as your IP address, as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Ratings
After a few days of play the app may ask whether you would like to rate it, using Apple's built-in rating prompt. Apple handles this; the app receives nothing.

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

## Contact
Questions or requests: [open an issue](https://github.com/OuadieZerhouni/app-configs/issues).
