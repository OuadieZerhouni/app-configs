# Tumbloc — Help & feedback

**How do I play?**
Swipe in any direction (or tap the arrow buttons) to tip the block over. The block is twice as tall as it is wide: it can stand upright or lie down. Get it **standing upright** on the hole to finish the level.

**Why did the block fall?**
Every part of the block must rest on a tile. If any part hangs over the edge — or over a bridge that is not there — it tumbles. No harm done: the game rewinds your last move.

**What do the special tiles do?**
- **Glass** tiles hold the block lying down, but shatter if you stand on them.
- **Round switches** fire whenever any part of the block touches them.
- **Cross switches** fire only when the block stands upright on them.
- Switches extend, retract or toggle **bridges**. A dotted outline shows where a bridge can appear.

**What are stars?**
Each level has a *par*: the fewest moves that solve it. Match par for three stars. Undo and hints are free and unlimited — stars are just for fun.

**I'm stuck.**
Tap the light bulb for the best next move from where you are.
