# Raft Battle — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 September 2026_

**In short: Raft Battle does not collect, store or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. You can play the whole game offline.

## Data stored on your device
Your game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, unlocked throwables and hats) and your settings (music, sound effects, haptics, difficulty, aiming aid) are saved locally on your device. They are never uploaded, and they are deleted when you delete the app. You can also erase them at any time with *Settings → Reset progress*.

## Network
When online, the game downloads one small public configuration file from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) that can adjust game balance and show announcements. This is the only network request the game makes. It contains no personal information and no identifiers, and nothing about you or your play is ever sent back. GitHub may process standard request data such as your IP address, as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Ratings
The game may occasionally show Apple's standard "rate this app" prompt. That prompt is provided by iOS; the game does not see whether or how you rate it.

## Children
The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

## Contact
