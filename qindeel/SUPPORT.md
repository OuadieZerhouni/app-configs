# Qindeel (قنديل): Help & feedback

**I found a mistake in a story.**
Please tell us: [open an issue](https://github.com/OuadieZerhouni/app-configs/issues) and name the story, the episode and the sentence. Every paragraph has its references in the app (the Sources button in the player), so a correction can be checked and published quickly.

**Where do the stories come from?**
From the Quran first, then authentic hadith, then established classical works such as Ibn Kathir's. Doubtful reports are left out. In the app, open Settings → Sources and method. Verses are copied without change from the Tanzil Project text (tanzil.net).

**Why is there no recitation of the verses?**
The verses are shown on screen exactly as written. They are never read by a synthetic voice, because a machine does not recite correctly.

**Why are the prophets not shown in the pictures?**
On purpose. The pictures show places, skies and objects only.

**I can't hear the narration.**
Check the volume and that the episode is playing (the large button in the player). The narration plays even with the silent switch on. If an episode says its narration is being prepared, it can still be read.

**The narration stops when a verse appears.**
That is the "Pause for verses" setting, so the verse can be read. Tap the verse to continue at once, or turn the setting off in Settings. With the screen locked the narration never stops for verses.

**How do I get all the stories?**
The first three stories, the first episode of every story and all the stories for the young are free. Qindeel Complete is a single purchase that opens the rest, including stories added later. There is no subscription. Bought it on another device? Settings → Restore purchase.

**How do I stop the daily reminder?**
Settings → Daily reminder.

**Contact**
A bug or an idea? [Open an issue](https://github.com/OuadieZerhouni/app-configs/issues).
