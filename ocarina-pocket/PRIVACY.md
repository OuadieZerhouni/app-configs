# Ocarina Pocket — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 September 2026_

**In short: Ocarina Pocket does not collect, store or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Microphone
The app uses the microphone only when you turn on the tuner, blow-to-play, or the “My ocarina” play-along mode. The sound is analysed live on your device to detect pitch and breath. Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. You can revoke microphone access at any time in your device settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

## Data stored on your device
Your preferences (ocarina type, glaze, sound settings), favourite songs and the tabs you write are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

## Network
When online, the app downloads a small public configuration file from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) to receive new songs, announcements and settings. This request contains no personal information. GitHub may process standard request data such as your IP address, as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
