# Wuff — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

**In short: Wuff does not collect, store or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Data stored on your device
Wuff saves your puppy's name, its bond level, trick stars, the coats and accessories you picked, the journal moments you found, how many days you visited, and your sound and vibration settings. It is stored only on your device, never uploaded, and deleted when you delete the app or tap "Start over" in Settings.

## Motion sensor
Wuff reads the phone's motion sensor only while the app is open, to notice when you shake the phone (your puppy gets dizzy). The readings are used instantly on the device and never stored or sent anywhere.

## Microphone and camera
Wuff does not use the microphone or the camera. The bark board only plays sounds; it does not listen.

## Network
When online, the app downloads a small public configuration file from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) to receive seasonal items, announcements and settings. This request contains no personal information. GitHub may process standard request data such as your IP address, as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
