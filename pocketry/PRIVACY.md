# Pocketry — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

**In short: Pocketry does not collect, store or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Data stored on your device
Pocketry stores the game files you add, your in-game saves and their history, your save states and their thumbnails, your cheat codes, and your settings (skin, theme, controller mapping). All of it stays on your device, in the app's own folder, which you can see in the Files app. Nothing is uploaded by Pocketry. It is deleted when you delete the app. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple backs up the app's folder with the rest of your device, under your Apple Account.

## Files you choose to share
When you use "Export" or "Share", Pocketry hands the file you picked (a save, a state or a backup archive) to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice.

## Purchases
Pocketry Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. Pocketry receives only the confirmation that the purchase exists; it never sees your payment details.

## Controllers
Game controllers are read only while the app is open, to play the game. Nothing about them is stored or sent.

## Network
When online, the app downloads a small public configuration file from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) to receive announcements and settings. This request contains no personal information. GitHub may process standard request data such as your IP address, as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Pocketry never downloads games.

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
