# Wuff — Help & feedback

**How do I play with my puppy?**
Tap its head for a happy yip, stroke the head or rub along a floppy ear, boop the nose, tickle under the chin, or hold your finger still for a snuggle. Five quick taps start the zoomies. There are 24 moments to find in the Journal (the book button).

**How does the bark board work?**
Tap **Talk** and press any of the 9 pads: your puppy acts out each bark, howl or squeak. Playing them to a real dog is just for fun; the app does not translate dog language.

**How do tricks and stars work?**
Tap **Tricks** and pick one. Your puppy does not always get it right, but it gets better with every star. One success per trick counts each day, and two counted days earn a star. A star opens the next trick. Give a treat right after a trick for a "Good pup!" bonus.

**How do I unlock coats and accessories?**
Open **Dress up**: every locked item says exactly how to earn it, such as visiting on more days, growing your bond, starring tricks, or finding journal moments. Seasonal hats appear around Halloween and the winter holidays. Everything is free.

**My puppy won't eat.**
After 7 treats in an hour your puppy is full. Try again a little later; nothing is lost.

**I can't hear any sounds.**
Check that Sound is on in Settings and that your phone's silent switch is off. Wuff's sounds mix with your music and follow the silent switch on purpose.
