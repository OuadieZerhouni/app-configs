# Boop! — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

**In short: Boop! does not collect, store or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking.

## Data stored on your device
Your boop counts, unlocked critters, streak, best scores and settings (sound, haptics, motion, language) are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, and they are deleted when you delete the app or use *Settings → Reset progress*.

## Network
The game works fully offline. When online, the app downloads a small public configuration file from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) at most every few hours, to receive settings and occasional announcements. This request contains no personal information and no identifiers. GitHub may process standard request data such as your IP address, as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Ratings
If you choose to rate the app, Apple's own rating sheet is shown. Boop! does not see who rated or what was written.

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
