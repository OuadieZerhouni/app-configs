# Mofu — Help & feedback

**How do I play with Mofu?**
Tap it for a boing, stroke its fur to pet it, press and hold for a squish (hold longer for a hug), and hold then drag to pick it up and toss it. Shake your phone to give it a wobble.

**How does Mofu get fluffier?**
Petting adds a little fluff; the brush (bottom-left button) adds more. Your first brush of each day gives a bonus. Fluff never goes down, even if you are away for a while.

**How do I unlock new fur colours and accessories?**
Open the Wardrobe: every locked item says exactly how to earn it — visiting on more days, reaching a fluff level, discovering reactions in the Journal, or sharing snacks. Everything is free.

**Mofu won't eat!**
Drag a snack from the snack tray right onto Mofu's mouth. After six snacks in an hour Mofu is full — try again a little later.

**I can't hear any sounds.**
Check that Sounds is on in Settings and that your phone's silent switch is off. Mofu's sounds mix with your music and follow the silent switch on purpose.
