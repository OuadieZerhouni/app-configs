# Hot Cold Globe — Help & feedback

**How do I play?**
Every day there is one hidden country. Type any country and press *Guess*. Your guess lights up on the globe: the warmer the colour, the closer it is to the hidden country. Keep guessing until you find it.

**How is "close" measured?**
By the shortest distance between the two countries' borders. Countries that share a border show *Shares a border*.

**When does the daily puzzle change?**
At midnight in your local time. Everyone gets the same country on the same date.

**What is the hint?**
After a few guesses you can reveal the continent of the hidden country. Using it is shown in your shared result.

**My streak reset.**
A streak counts days in a row where you solved the daily puzzle. Missing a day or giving up resets it.

**The colours are hard to tell apart.**
Open *Settings → Colours* and choose *Colour-blind friendly*.

**Why is a territory missing / named differently?**
Country names and borders come from Natural Earth (public domain). If something looks wrong, tell us below.
