# Qindeel (قنديل): Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

**In short: Qindeel does not collect, store or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Data stored on your device
Your listening progress, favourites, the days on which you listened, your settings (text size, kids mode, reminder time and similar) and whether you bought Qindeel Complete. It is stored only on your device, never uploaded, and deleted when you delete the app.

## Notifications
If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules one notification a day on your device. It is not a push notification: nothing is sent from a server, and no token leaves your phone. You can turn it off in the app's Settings at any time.

## Purchases
Qindeel Complete is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. The app only learns from Apple whether this Apple ID owns it. We never see your payment details.

## Network
When online, the app downloads a small public configuration file from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) to receive announcements and settings, and, if more stories are offered as downloads, the story files you choose to download. These requests contain no personal information. GitHub may process standard request data such as your IP address, as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Links you tap (the hadith source pages on sunnah.com, tanzil.net, this page) open in your browser and are governed by those sites' own policies.

## Sharing
When you share an episode, the picture and text go only to the app you pick in the system share sheet.

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children. It is not listed in the Kids category.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

## Contact
Questions or requests: [open an issue](https://github.com/OuadieZerhouni/app-configs/issues).
