# Boop! — Help & feedback

**How do I play?**
Tap the critter right on its nose — that's a boop! Every boop counts towards your daily goal and towards unlocking new friends. Tapping the body makes it wobble, holding it gives a hug, and dragging gives it a squish.

**What are combos?**
Boop quickly without pausing for more than a second. 10 boops in a row is a *Boop-a-doop*, 25 a *Snoot Storm*, 50 a *MEGA BOOP*. Keep going and your critter may sneeze.

**How do I unlock new critters?**
New friends join as your lifetime boops grow. The *Critters* tab shows how many boops are left until the next one. There are twelve in total.

**How does the streak work?**
Reach the daily goal (shown by the ring at the top) to keep your streak. Missing a whole day resets it. The day changes at midnight in your local time.

**What is Boop Rush?**
A 30-second round: critters pop up from nine bushes — boop as many as you can. Golden ones are worth extra. Sleepy ones (eyes closed) want to nap: booping them costs points and breaks your streak.

**The sound is too loud / I want silence.**
*Settings → Sound* and *Settings → Haptics* turn each off. Boop! also respects the silent switch.

**Too much movement on screen?**
Turn on *Settings → Reduce motion* for a calmer screen with fewer effects.

**Can I change the language?**
Yes: *Settings → Language*. Available in English, العربية, Deutsch, Español, Français, 日本語 and Português.

**I lost my progress.**
Progress is stored only on your device, so it does not move to a new phone or survive deleting the app. We collect nothing, so we can't restore it either — sorry!

**Are there ads or purchases?**
No. Boop! has no ads and no in-app purchases.
