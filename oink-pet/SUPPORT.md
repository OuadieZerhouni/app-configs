# Oink — Help & feedback

**How do I take care of my piglet?**
Watch the four meters at the top — Tummy, Clean, Fun and Energy. Tap *Feed* and drag a snack onto your piglet, tap *Bath* and scrub the mud with your finger before you rinse, tap *Play* for Apple Catch, and tap *Sleep* when it gets tired.

**Can my piglet get sick or run away?**
Never. If you are away for a while it gets hungry, muddy or bored and tells you so, but it is always waiting for you.

**Does my piglet change while the app is closed?**
Yes, slowly — just like a real pet. Needs go down over real time, and a sleeping piglet keeps resting and wakes up when it is fully rested.

**How do I get acorns?**
Claim the daily gift (the present button), play Apple Catch, and care for your piglet when it really needs it. Acorns buy hats, glasses, scarves and new rooms in the *Closet*. There is no way to buy acorns with real money.

**How does my piglet grow?**
Caring earns experience. At level 5 your piglet becomes a little pig, and at level 10 a big pig. New snacks and outfits unlock as it levels up.

**Seasonal items?**
Some snacks and hats appear only around Halloween and the winter holidays. Anything you bought stays yours forever.

**The sounds are off / my phone is on silent.**
Oink respects the silent switch. You can also turn sounds and vibration on or off in *Settings*.

**I want a brand-new piglet.**
*Settings → Start over with a new piglet.* This cannot be undone.
