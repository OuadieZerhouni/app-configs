# Raft Battle — Help & feedback

**How do I throw?**
Touch the screen and drag *backwards*, like pulling a slingshot. The direction sets the angle and the length sets the power. Let go to throw. Watch the wind arrow at the top — it pushes your throw left or right.

**I keep missing!**
Turn on *Settings → Easy aim* to see the full flight path, or lower the difficulty. Crates and barrels block throws; break them first or lob over them.

**How do I get new throwables and hats?**
Win battles to earn coins, then spend them in the Shop. Everything is unlocked with coins you earn in the game — there is nothing to buy with real money.

**Can two people play?**
Yes: choose *2-Player* on the title screen and pass the phone between turns.

**I lost my progress.**
Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app, or using *Settings → Reset progress*, erases it.
